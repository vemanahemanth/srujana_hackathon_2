Mathematical Formula Specifications

Historical VaR (Positive Loss Convention):

$\text{VaR}_\alpha = \text{Quantile}_{1-\alpha}(R) \times (-1)$. A positive VaR represents potential loss fraction.

Coherent Expected Shortfall (Acerbi & Tasche 2002):

Calculates discrete tail expectation with exact fractional weighting. Mathematically satisfies sub-additivity: $\text{ES}_\alpha \ge \text{VaR}_\alpha$.

Drawdown Dynamics:

Tracks ongoing vs fully-recovered drawdowns, calculating peak-to-trough duration and recovery duration.

Assumptions & Critical Limitations

Important Caveat: Historical risk metrics reflect past sample dispersion and empirical quantiles. They do NOT guarantee future downside protection, stationarity, or liquidity under regime shifts.
  • Assumes independent identically distributed (i.i.d.) returns over the sample window.
  • Does not account for non-linear option convexities or liquidity gaps.
  • Annualization factor assumes 252 discrete trading days per calendar year.

Why Fragility Analysis Matters

Conventional risk engines produce single-point estimates (e.g. "VaR is 2.3%"). The Fragility Lab challenges this false certainty by applying systematic parameter perturbations (lookback window shifts, alternative volatility estimators, transaction friction, and confidence shifts) and ranking which modeling choices have the greatest impact on reported risk.

Statistical Safeguards & Notices

Statistical Limitation: Failure to reject the null hypothesis ($p \ge 0.05$) does NOT prove the model is correct. It merely indicates empirical compatibility with nominal breach probability under the tested sample size.

First-order Markov property evaluates lag-1 transition clustering. Small breach samples ($n_{11}=0$) naturally limit test power to detect multi-period volatility clustering.

Security & Containment Boundaries

• In-Process Execution: Model plugins execute strictly in-process with caller permissions. Untrusted arbitrary code execution is prevented.

• Path Traversal Defense: Filename inputs are strictly sanitized via basename extraction to eliminate filesystem traversal vulnerabilities.

• Finite Numerical Arithmetic: Engine rejects non-finite IEEE 754 floats (NaN and Inf) at input validation boundaries.

• Strict Scope Boundary: No live market trading credentials, Upstox tokens, or cloud storage connections are embedded or active in Phase 9.
